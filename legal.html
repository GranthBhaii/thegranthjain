Privacy & credits

Your privacy, and who made what.

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies, and it doesn’t collect anything about you unless you choose to contact me.

Privacy notice

In short: no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies. This site doesn’t store anything on your device. The only outside services it touches are Google Fonts (for the typefaces), a live preview of hellmc.in if you open the Web Development page, and a form-delivery service if you send a message through the contact form.

Who is responsible

Granth Jain, an independent freelance developer based in India. You can reach me at granthjain694@gmail.com.

What this site stores on your device

Nothing. It sets no cookies and uses no local storage. If you switch between light and dark mode with the rope, that choice only lasts until you close or reload the page.

Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). When a page loads, your browser asks Google’s servers for the font files, so Google receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type. That is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. I don’t receive any of that information.

Live preview of another site

The Web Development page shows a live preview of hellmc.in inside the page. It is only loaded once you open that page. hellmc.in is run separately, and its own privacy policy applies to anything it does while it is displayed. Other links on this site (for example to my Discord community) take you to other companies’ websites, where their policies apply.

When you contact me

You can reach me in two ways. The “Hire me” buttons open your own email app with my address filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. The “Hire me” enquiry form on the home page sends what you enter into it (the options you choose, such as the service, budget range and start date, plus your name, email address, your phone number and company or website if you add them, and your message) to me by email. The form is delivered through Web3Forms, which processes that information on my behalf only to pass it on to my inbox. A copy of the enquiry is also posted to a private channel on a Discord server that I run (Discord Inc., USA), through a small function on my hosting provider (Vercel), so that I see it quickly. Nothing is sent until you press “Send message”.

I use what you send only to reply to you and to discuss your project. I don’t add you to any mailing list or share your details with anyone else. I keep it only as long as it is useful for our work together, and I’ll delete it if you ask.

The hosting provider

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting company, whose servers briefly process technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. That is governed by the hosting provider’s own privacy policy. I don’t use those logs to identify visitors.

Your rights

You can ask me what personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, by emailing me. I’ll reply as soon as I reasonably can.
